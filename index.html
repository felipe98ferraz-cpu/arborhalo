Your Tree, Lit From Every Single Side

At dusk your tree turns into a shadow.

Lumina Garden brings it back.

The ring wraps the trunk and throws warm light upward from all around it, so the whole tree glows from every side.

Branches gain depth. The trunk looks like a column, not a cutout.

With the optional solar panel, it lights itself every night, on its own.

Aluminum alloy body, built to sit outside through the season

  • Your house stops disappearing at 5pm. Guests pull in and the tree is glowing. Warm light from every side makes the front of your home look intentional.
  • Lights itself, every night, without you. With the solar setup, it charges in the sun all day and comes on when the light drops. No switch to flip. No timer to set. No app.
  • One tree, lit all the way around. A stake spot lights one face and leaves the rest dark. Lumina Garden circles the trunk, lighting it from every side.
  • Aluminum alloy body built for the yard. It’s made to stay outside and keep looking like it belongs there.
